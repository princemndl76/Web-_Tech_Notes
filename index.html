🎓 EXAM & QUESTION GUIDE

Bloom Level Key

Understand what each Bloom level means and identify the type of question asked in your examination.

Level Meaning Typical Question Words
L1 Remember Recall facts Remember basic information and definitions.
Define List Name State
L2 Understand Explain ideas Show that you understand the concept.
Explain Describe Differentiate
L3 Apply Use in a new case Apply knowledge to solve a problem.
Write Show Solve Implement
L4 Analyze Break down and compare Examine parts, relationships and differences.
Compare Analyze Distinguish
L5 Evaluate Judge and justify Give a reasoned judgment or assessment.
Justify Assess Evaluate
L6 Create Design something new Create or propose a new solution or idea.
Design Propose Develop
🧠
Exam Tip

Higher Bloom levels usually require deeper thinking. Questions using compare, analyze, justify, design generally demand more detailed answers.
